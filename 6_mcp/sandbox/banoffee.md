# Classic Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Overview
Banoffee Pie is a classic British dessert that combines bananas, toffee (caramel), and cream on a biscuit crust. The name is a portmanteau of "banana" and "toffee." This indulgent dessert is surprisingly simple to make and always impresses guests.

## Ingredients

### For the Crust
- 250g digestive biscuits (graham crackers)
- 100g unsalted butter, melted
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar (optional)

### For the Toffee/Caramel Filling
- 100g unsalted butter
- 100g dark brown sugar
- 1 x 397g can of sweetened condensed milk

### For the Topping
- 3-4 ripe bananas
- 300ml double cream (heavy cream)
- 1 tablespoon icing sugar (optional)
- Dark chocolate shavings or cocoa powder for decoration
- Fresh mint leaves (optional garnish)

## Equipment
- 9-inch pie tin or springform pan
- Food processor or rolling pin and plastic bag
- Medium saucepan
- Electric mixer or whisk
- Spatula
- Measuring cups and spoons

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Crust
1. Place digestive biscuits in a food processor and pulse until fine crumbs form. Alternatively, place biscuits in a sealed plastic bag and crush with a rolling pin.
2. Mix biscuit crumbs with melted butter (and sugar if using) until well combined.
3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch pie tin or springform pan.
4. Chill in refrigerator for at least 30 minutes to set.

### Step 2: Make the Toffee Filling
1. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.
2. Add the dark brown sugar and stir until dissolved.
3. Pour in the sweetened condensed milk and bring to a gentle boil, stirring constantly.
4. Continue cooking for 5-7 minutes, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens and turns a golden caramel color.
5. Remove from heat and let cool for 5 minutes.
6. Pour the warm toffee over the chilled biscuit base and spread evenly.
7. Return to refrigerator and chill for at least 1 hour until the toffee is set.

### Step 3: Prepare the Topping
1. Whip the double cream with icing sugar (if using) until soft peaks form.
2. Slice the bananas into ¼-inch thick rounds.
3. Arrange banana slices evenly over the set toffee layer.
4. Spread or pipe the whipped cream over the bananas.
5. Decorate with dark chocolate shavings, cocoa powder, or mint leaves.

## Tips and Variations

### Pro Tips
- Use ripe but firm bananas for best results
- Chill the bowl and beaters before whipping cream for better volume
- For a darker caramel, cook the toffee mixture a minute longer
- Let the pie chill for at least 2 hours before serving for cleaner slices

### Variations
- **Chocolate Base**: Add 2 tablespoons cocoa powder to the biscuit crust
- **Salted Caramel**: Add ½ teaspoon sea salt to the toffee mixture
- **Coffee Infused**: Add 1 teaspoon instant coffee to the whipped cream
- **Nutty Twist**: Add 50g chopped toasted pecans or walnuts to the crust
- **Vegan Version**: Use vegan biscuits, coconut oil instead of butter, coconut cream for whipping, and vegan condensed milk

## Storage
- Store in refrigerator for up to 3 days
- Best served chilled
- Add bananas just before serving if preparing ahead (to prevent browning)

## Nutritional Information (per serving, serves 8)
- Calories: ~450 kcal
- Fat: 30g
- Carbohydrates: 45g
- Protein: 5g
- Sugar: 35g

## History and Origin
Banoffee Pie was invented in 1972 at The Hungry Monk restaurant in Jevington, East Sussex, England. The original recipe was created by chef Ian Dowding and restaurant owner Nigel Mackenzie. The dessert gained widespread popularity in the UK during the 1990s and has since become a beloved classic worldwide.

## Serving Suggestions
- Serve with a drizzle of extra caramel sauce
- Pair with a cup of strong coffee or tea
- Add a scoop of vanilla ice cream on the side
- Garnish with fresh berries for contrast

## Troubleshooting
- **Runny toffee**: Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes next time
- **Soggy crust**: Ensure crust is thoroughly chilled before adding toffee
- **Bananas browning**: Brush with lemon juice or assemble just before serving
- **Cream not whipping**: Ensure cream is cold and bowl/beaters are chilled

Enjoy this decadent British classic that perfectly balances sweet toffee, fresh bananas, and light cream on a crunchy biscuit base!
