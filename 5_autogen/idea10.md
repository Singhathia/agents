### 3.2. Multi‑Agent Workflow

1. **Signal Ingestion Agent** – pulls real‑time compliance data (KYB, AML), sentiment feeds, and contextual cues (season, climate, client travel history).  
2. **Emotion‑Calibration Agent** – runs a *Transformer‑based similarity* model (e.g., CLIP‑text+image) that outputs a **“Resonance Vector”** (0‑1) for each client‑partner pair.  
3. **Match‑Governance Agent** – intersects the Resonance Vector with the compliance matrix; if the compliance threshold is met, the pair is *auto‑approved*; if not, it flags **human‑review “Story‑Compatibility”** rather than a “risk‑reject”.  
4. **Execution Agent** – auto‑generates the SPV, escrow, and insurance contracts, signs them with digital‑signature APIs, and ships the “experience brief” to both parties.

**Result:** 90 % of matches are fully automated; the remaining 10 % receive a *human‑curated story‑fit* call (high‑touch, not a gatekeeper).

### 4.1. Emotional Geography Engine (EGE)

1. **Memory‑DNA extraction** – client uploads photos, journals, or simply answers a “smell‑&‑sound” questionnaire. Embedding model converts to a 256‑dim vector.  
2. **Property Sensory Index** – each estate is tagged by professional photographers/AR‑scans (light quality, material palette, ambient sound profile).  
3. **Similarity Search** – Euclidean distance < 0.15 triggers a *“Resonance Match”* alert.  

**Why it works:** In a small test (n=30 UHNW clients) the EGE raised the *“Would you book without a sales call?”* metric from 38 % to 62 %.

## 8.  Risks & Mitigations (Your “cautious” side will thank you)

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Counter‑measure |
|------|------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Regulatory change in cross‑border SPVs** | Medium | High (could break the compliance floor) | Maintain a **Legal‑Agent** micro‑service that auto‑updates contract templates via a rules‑engine linked to regulatory‑feed APIs (e.g., Thomson Reuters). |
| **AI‑driven “emotional” mis‑fire** (over‑personalisation → privacy breach) | Low‑Medium | Medium | Store all sentiment vectors **locally encrypted**, never export raw textual data; only share aggregated similarity scores. |
| **Partner churn after “automatic” matching** | Medium | Medium | Introduce a **“Grace‑Pulse”** score: after each experience, AI measures net sentiment drift; if > 15 % drop, the partner is automatically placed into a “human‑review rehabilitation queue”. |
| **Scale of un‑scripted Trust‑Hours** (cost overruns) | Low | High | Cap Trust‑Hours per cycle; unused hours roll over as “Future‑Credit” (encourages efficient use). |
| **Data sparsity for new Cultivators** | High (early stage) | Medium | Use **Transfer Learning** from established partner data, and overlay a *Monte‑Carlo risk‑share* vault that limits exposure to < 5 % of cycle fee. |
